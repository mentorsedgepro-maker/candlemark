02 — How it works

From sign-up to your first copied trade

Step 01

Create and verify your account

Register with your email, then confirm it with a six-digit code.

Step 02

Complete identity verification

Submit your profile and ID so withdrawals can be enabled once reviewed.

Step 03

Fund your account

Send a crypto deposit and upload proof. Funds are credited after verification.

Step 04

Choose a mentor or trade yourself

Compare mentor profiles, set an allocation, or place your own trades with stop loss and take profit.

Step 05

Stay in control

Track positions and your ledger live, pause or stop copies, and request withdrawals when you choose.
